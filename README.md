# IntegrityWorks 🚀

### **The Trusted Marketplace for Elite Developers & Serious Clients.**

Tired of the freelance chaos? IntegrityWorks is a curated platform that eliminates scams, low-quality projects, and unreliable talent. We ensure a fair, transparent, and professional environment where **skilled developers** find meaningful work and **serious clients** get exceptional results.

---

## ✨ Why IntegrityWorks?

### For Developers: Code with Confidence.

**Your skills are in demand. Your time is valuable. Stop sifting through scams.**

- **✅ Vetted, High-Quality Projects:** Every job post is reviewed for technical feasibility, clear scope, and **fair market rates.** No more lowball offers.
- **🚫 No Spec Work:** We prohibit "complete this test for free" scams. Your portfolio and experience should speak for itself.
- **💳 Secure Payments & Milestones:** Built-in escrow and milestone payments ensure **you get paid** for your committed work. No more payment nightmares.
- **👥 A Community of Peers:** Connect with clients who respect your craft and are ready to collaborate.

> **Join a platform built to protect and value your expertise.**

### For Clients: Hire with Certainty.

**You need talent you can trust. You need projects delivered right.**

- **🔒 Access a Vetted Talent Pool:** Hire from a curated community of professional developers. **No more unqualified bids.**
- **📋 Clear & Structured Hiring:** Post your project with clear requirements and a realistic budget. We help you attract the right talent from the start.
- **🛡️ Risk-Free Collaboration:** Use our milestone-based payment system to **stay in control.** Pay for progress only when you approve the work.
- **⚡️ Save Time & Reduce Overhead:** Skip the lengthy, unreliable hiring process. Find proven talent ready to start on your critical projects.

> **Join a platform where quality and reliability are guaranteed.**

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

We leverage a modern, scalable, and secure technology stack to ensure a premium experience:

- **Frontend:** Angular 17+, TypeScript, RxJS, Angular Material
- **Backend:** ASP.NET Core Web API, Entity Framework Core
- **Database:** Azure Cosmos DB
- **Authentication & Security:** Azure Active Directory, Azure Key Vault
- **Cloud & Deployment:** Azure App Service, GitHub Actions CI/CD
- **Testing:** Karma & Jasmine (Frontend), xUnit (Backend)

## 🚀 Getting Started

### For Developers

1.  **Sign Up:** Create your developer profile to showcase your skills and portfolio.
2.  **Get Verified:** Our team reviews your profile to ensure quality standards.
3.  **Browse Works:** Explore vetted projects that match your expertise.
4.  **Submit Proposals:** Connect with clients who value your work.
5.  **Get Paid Securely:** Work through protected milestones and receive timely payments.

### For Clients

1.  **Post a Project:** Submit your project details, scope, and budget.
2.  **Review Talent:** Receive proposals from our curated pool of developers.
3.  **Collaborate Securely:** Use our platform to manage milestones, communicate, and share files.
4.  **Approve & Pay:** Review completed work and release payments with ease.
